How to get the numbers in:
  1. In the ChargePoint app or website, open Activity → Monthly Statement for a charger.
  2. Select the month, then select & copy the whole statement (the rows with dates and kWh).
  3. Pick that charger below, choose the year, paste, and tap Import.
Do this once per charger per month. New sessions add on; duplicates are skipped automatically.
