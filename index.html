Wildlife safety · Important

Always Carry Bear Spray

We are in bear country. Bear spray is your most effective defense — more reliable than a firearm at close range.

Check the cabinPrevious guest may have left a can — check closets & entry
Buy locallySam Patch Co. · Grocery & Sundries
Also availableHigh Mountain Adventures · Yale Creek Gas Station (Hwy 20 & Yale-Kilgore) · Most outdoor shops
How to useRemove safety clip · Aim low · Spray within 60 feet
Bear encounterStay calm · Don't run · Back away slowly

Never hike alone
without bear spray

Yellowstone averages 1-3 bear
encounters per week in season

Fire safety · Know before you burn

Fire Pit & Campfire Rules

Always check idl.idaho.gov/fire-management/fire-restrictions-finder
Fire pit rules Never leave unattended · Fully extinguish before bed · Keep water nearby
Stage 1 restriction Campfires only in cabin-provided fire structures on private land
Stage 2 restriction No campfires at all — propane only
Total ban No open flames of any kind including propane fire pits
Report a fire 911 · or · 1-800-632-8000
